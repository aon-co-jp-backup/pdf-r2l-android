PDF R2L Android is an Android GUI application with an APK that changes the
binding direction (right-to-left "R2L" / left-to-right "L2R") of PDF files.

It is a Rust reimplementation of "pdf-r2l" by ottijp
(https://github.com/ottijp/pdf-r2l), which is licensed under the GNU Affero
General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0). This program is therefore also

available at https://github.com/aon-co-jp/pdf-r2l-android . If you modify this
